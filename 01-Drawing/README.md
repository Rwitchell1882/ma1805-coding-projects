My emoji was chosen due to my love of horror and, more specifically, zombie movies, due to the cultural commentary presented by directors like George A. Romero in his trilogy of the dead and its themes of oppressive hierarchies both pre- and post-apocalyptic collapse. Creating a zombie also allowed me to get creative with my emoji. I was able to design a missing nose and an eye socket, as well as using white ellipses to portray sunken cheeks.
